## Limits

These are presentation models, not manufacturing CAD. Unlabelled gauges, radii, fasteners, box dimensions and tolerances are estimated. The representative 150 mm-wide section is not a rating-validated section. Copper/aluminium selection changes appearance only; it does not calculate capacity or conductor size. Five terminal blades do not establish phase order or PE sizing.

Hidden transposition routing, expansion links and breaker internals are omitted where not established by the images. Ordinary bend conductor routes and tap-off contacts are schematic. IP covers show the pictured arrangement, not certified sealing performance. Installation, thermal, short-circuit, support-load and clearance approval requires current manufacturer engineering documentation.
