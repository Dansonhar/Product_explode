## Proposed creative direction — recommendations, not product facts

Start with one Linkk-approved sandwich busduct section and its connection/joint. ProductViu should offer rotate/zoom, controlled aligned explosion, clickable approved part names, section view and reassembly. Add a tap-off unit as a second demonstration, followed by NSPB once drawings are supplied. Never invent hidden geometry or treat AI-generated internals as factual CAD.

Reuse an approved model and part dictionary across four outputs:
- ProductViu: interactive product learning and sales presentation.
- Website concept: product families, applications, technical downloads and enquiry journey.
- Video: realistic assembled-to-exploded sequence, two detail moments and an approved end state.
- Social: short vertical/square feature stories and cutaways, kept private during the proposal.

Likely audiences to validate: electrical consultants, M&E contractors, data-centre teams, industrial/building owners and distributors. The business hypothesis is better product understanding and more useful enquiries; no conversion/performance promise is established yet.
